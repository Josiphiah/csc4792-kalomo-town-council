DISTRICT ADMINISTRATION
Kalomo District, the District Commissioner oversees government and local authority programs,
ensuring their effective implementation to drive development.
The Local Authority is responsible for maintaining law and order while collaborating with key
departments such as Health, Forestry, Agriculture, Social Welfare, Community Development, and
Livestock and Fisheries.
Additionally, the Local Authority plays a crucial role in infrastructure development, environmental
management, and economic planning, working closely with stakeholders to enhance service
delivery and promote sustainable growth

KALOMO DISTRICT DAIRY COOPERATIVE SOCIETY
LIMITED (KDDCSL)
1. PROJECT SUMMARY DESCRIPTION
Kalomo District Dairy Cooperative Society Limited (KDCSL) has a membership of 1526 farmers. It is
located in the central business center of Kalomo whose mandate is to supply dairy products to
different institutions or business entities like dairy gold Zambia limited and others. It is a registered
cooperative society.
2. PROJECT STRENGTH
· It has a reliable membership of 1,526 members owning dairy animals
· Members have knowledge on cattle rearing.
· Possess Land, infrastructure and equipments.
· Located near the Great North Road.
· Due to the growing population and increase in dairy supply, the support from kalomo community
and have opened two solar powered milk satelite centre in Bbelo and Rural Resettlement (RR)
· Has a Board and trained staff.

KALOMO MILIMO MULTI PURPOSE
COOPERATIVE
PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Kalomo Milimo Multi-purpose Co-operative Society Ltd was established in 2023 and and has a
mandate of milling company, processing maize into mealie meal. The cooperative society has a
milling plant which was sourced from Citizen Economic Empowerment Commission in 2024.
PROJECT STRENGTHS
Available raw materials
Assets-Land, Milling plant, truck
Government support
Community ownership
Employment creation
DESCRIPTION OF FUTURE PRODUCTION
the cooperative has plans to increase its production and also add value to the products by branding
and packaging, improve production and quality of the product.

MARKET ANALYSIS
There is available market for both local and international market, the market base has not been
saturated at the moment for both human consumption and livestock feed as the cooperative is
dealing with staple food that is consumed everyday. With the government policy on irrigated
agriculture the cooperative is guaranteed of constant supply even in drought seasons.
AVAILABILITY OF TECHNOLOGY
The cooperative acquired a top of technology milling plant that has a capacity to mill over 200 tonnes
per day and which provides environmental friendly aspects to both the user and the community,
however there is still room to improve on the part of packaging and branding. This may require some
equipment and training of personnel in order to meet this objective.
AVAILABILITY OF INFRATRUCTURE
Available infrastructure is not adequate and not to the standards that will meet the set objectives.
There is need to set up state of art infrastructure on the site in order to boost consumer confidence.
These infrastructure will need to be well ventilated and sanitation in order to meet the international
standard of hygiene.
AVAILABILITY OF HUMAN RESOURCE AT THE LOCATION
The cooperative at the moment has a skeleton structure that might not meet the set standards,
therefore the cooperative will require assistance in milling management, packaging, branding,
processing and distribution as well as life skill training and business skills.
INVESTMENT REQUIRED AND PROFITABILITY ANALYSIS
The cooperative will require assistance in the following areas
1. Shed completion 6. Maize stocks
2. Borehole equipped with submissive pump 7. Land
3. Ablution block 8. Salaries
4. Electrical fittings 9. Technical assistance and trainings
5. Transformer setup 10. Working capital
All these will add upto 1.400,000 kwacha. With this investment the cooperative is able to raise a
gross profit of 26, 400,000 kwacha
