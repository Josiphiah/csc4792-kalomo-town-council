i. Revenue Performance:
The meeting observed that overall revenue performance for
2025 was satisfactory despite challenges in collecting some local
revenues. Stakeholders commended the efforts of the Council in
enhancing local revenue mobilization but urged continued
strengthening of collection mechanisms, especially for business
levies and property rates.
ii. Business Levy Collections:
It was noted that the over-performance under the business levy
was mainly due to arrears collected from the previous year.
Stakeholders recommended that the Council continue with
follow-ups on outstanding levies and consider updating the
business register to improve compliance.
iii. Other Income:
The Council had initially projected to generate K250,000 from
commercial ventures; however, due to ongoing renovations at
the Council Lodge, the income was revised downwards.
Stakeholders recommended expediting the renovation works to
ensure the facility resumes operations and contributes to
revenue in 2026.
6 | Page
iv. Expenditure Alignment:
The Director of Finance reported that expenditure estimates
were revised to align with the changes in revenue projections.
Stakeholders supported this approach and emphasized prudent
financial management to ensure value for money in all council
expenditures.
The Stakeholders NOTED the report of the Director Finance on the
presentation of 2025 budget performance/proposed 2026 budget.
CES/ 02/11/25: STATUS OF 2025 CDF, ZDSP AND ROADS GRANTS
PROJECTS BY DIRECTOR ENGINEERING
The Civil Engineer presented a detailed report to the Stakeholders
on the status of the 2025 CDF projects and the Roads Grants. The
report highlighted that Kalomo District was performing well in the
implementation and delivery of both CDF-funded projects and
those financed through the Roads Grants. It was noted that most
projects were progressing according to schedule, with improved
supervision and adherence to approved specifications.
In addition, the Stakeholders were sensitized on the Zambia
Devolution Support Programme (ZDSP). The Civil Engineer
explained the purpose of the programme, which is designed to
strengthen the capacity of local authorities in financial
management, service delivery, governance, and infrastructure
development. The session further clarified the assessment criteria
used to determine eligibility for the funding.
During the discussion, the Stakeholders raised several follow-up
questions for clarification. They asked for an update on which
specific projects under the 2025 CDF allocation had been
completed and which ones were still ongoing. They further inquired
about any challenges being experienced in implementing the Roads
Grants projects and whether any delays were anticipated on the
remaining works. Stakeholders also sought clarification on the
measures being taken by the Local Authority to ensure quality
control, adherence to engineering standards, and timely payments
to contractors.
The Stakeholders additionally asked what factors had contributed
to Kalomo District performing well in the delivery of both CDF and
Roads Grants projects, including how the district’s performance
compared with other districts. They were also interested in
7 | Page
understanding the lessons learned from the current cycle that could
enhance future project implementation.
Regarding the Zambia Devolution Support Programme (ZDSP),
Stakeholders asked for a clearer explanation of the key eligibility
criteria used during the assessment. They wanted to know the
specific areas where Kalomo had fallen short, as well as the
corrective measures the Local Authority planned to undertake to
improve its chances of qualifying in the next funding cycle. They
also asked about the benefits that districts receive when they
qualify for ZDSP and what opportunities Kalomo may have missed.
Further, the Stakeholders queried the role that Ward Development
Committees (WDCs) could play in enhancing Local Authority
performance, and how Stakeholders themselves could support
efforts to strengthen compliance, reporting, and overall service
delivery. They also asked whether additional capacity-building
initiatives or resources were required to help improve future
performance under both CDF management and ZDSP assessments.
The Stakeholders NOTED the report of the Ass. Director
Engineering Civil Engineer on the status of 2025 CDF, ZDSP and
Roads grants project
