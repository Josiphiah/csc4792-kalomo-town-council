CES/04/11/24: DIRECTOR FINANCE’S REPORT ON 2024 BUDGET
PERFORMANCE
The Director of Finance reported that Kalomo Town Council had
planned to collect a total of K9, 484, 368 in own source revenue for
the 4th quarter of 2024. He revealed further that as of the end of the
year, the council successfully collected K5, 731, 704 which
represented 60% of the quarterly own source revenue.
Concerning the National support he indicated that the council had
projected to receive K85, 914, 886.00; yet, as at the end of the 4th
Quater the actual amount which was received stood at K66, 868,
061.
It was then reported that the total income budget for the year 2024
was K95, 399, 254, against which the council had only managed to
collect K73, 051, 398 representing a 76% collection efficiency for the
stated year.

CES/05/11/25 PROPOSED 2025 BUDGET
The Director of Finance reported that the 2025 Budget Estimates for
Kalomo Town Council amount to K156.3 million, representing a 63
percent increase from the 2024 Budget Estimates of K95.4 million.
The increase was attributed to the inclusion of the Cash for Work
Fund amounting to K42.2 million, an increase in the Constituency
Development Fund from K61 million to K72 million, the incorporation
of budgets for Fisheries and Livestock and Agriculture under
devolved functions, and the inclusion of the Social Protection and
Community Development function.

The Director of Finance reported that, according to the budget
summary by Economic Classification, out of the total budget of
K156.3 million, Goods and Services have been allocated K68.8
million, representing 44 percent of the budget, to cover the
administrative component of the Constituency Development Fund
(CDF), Councillors’ monthly allowances, and other services. Non-
Financial Assets have been allocated K46 million, accounting for
29.4 percent of the budget, and include funding for community
projects under the CDF. Grants and Other Payments have been
allocated K24.9 million, representing 15.96 percent of the total
budget, which includes funds for Cash for Work activities under the
Cash for Work Fund. Personal Emoluments have been allocated
K14.8 million, representing 9.5 percent of the total budget, to cater
for salaries and wages for the council workforce. Current Liabilities
have been allocated K1.6 million to cater for the liquidation of
liabilities. The Director of Finance further reported that this
distribution ensures adequate funding for key operational areas,
community projects, personnel costs, and the settlement of financial
obligations

approved Kalomo Town Council Integrated Development Plan.

CES/06/11/24: Plenary
§ Members raised a concern that, there was need for the Council
Management to be strategic with priorities so that they should
slowly be procuring necessary Earth Moving Equipment/Machinery
such as Tipper Trucks and Front End Loader for easy of works,
construction of Dams and Maize Depots to accommodate farmers.

§ There was also a concern for the Council to ensure that they revamp

coming up with new structures and better still maintainance of the
Drive Inn to include a proper bar.
§ Another follow up submission was made on the need to introduce
the numbering of shops, booths and market stands and also ensure
that the Council attracts Serious business clients who could
eventually put up Shopping Malls in the district.
