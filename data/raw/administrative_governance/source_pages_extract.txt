SOURCE PAGE EXTRACTS - Kalomo Town Council administrative/governance dataset
Collected by: Nicholas

These notes record the source material used for the administrative and
governance dataset. The scraper targets the same Kalomo Town Council pages
listed below, but this working environment could not fetch the live site
reliably: direct automated requests returned HTTP 403, and hosted fetch tools
honour the site's robots.txt restrictions.

To keep the dataset verifiable, I compiled these notes from indexed copies of
the same pages, readable council news posts, and direct browser checks of the
live site where indexed text was incomplete. I have kept uncertainty visible:
if a page did not expose a detail clearly, the gap is noted here instead of
being filled from guesswork. Running the scraper from a normal campus or home
connection should still save the full HTML and extracted text into this folder.

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE: About Us (https://www.kalomocouncil.gov.zm/?page_id=118)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Kalomo served as the initial administrative capital of North-Western
Rhodesia, 1902-1907. Southern Province, along the Lusaka-Livingstone Road
(T1), ~120 km north of Livingstone, ~400 km south of Lusaka. Zimba District
Council separated from Kalomo in 2012 (under President Michael Chilufya
Sata). Economy: agriculture and livestock; district is the highest maize
producer in the country and has the second-largest cattle population in
the province.

------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE: Read more / district profile (https://www.kalomocouncil.gov.zm/?page_id=2242)
------------------------------------------------------------------
District population: 268,753 (2021 census figure as published on this
page; the homepage separately states 280,000 in a more general/rounded
context). Surface area: 8,374 km^2. Elevation: ~1,300 m above sea level.
Borders 5 districts: Itezhi-Tezhi (Central Province), Namwala, Choma,
Kazungula, Zimba (all Southern Province). District has a total of 20
Wards. Comprises 3 Chiefdoms (Chikanta, Siachitema, Sipatunyana) and 2
Constituencies (Kalomo Central, Dundumwezi).

PAGE: FAQS (https://www.kalomocouncil.gov.zm/?page_id=2259)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Defines CDF (Constituency Development Fund) - established 1995 under the
Local Government Act Cap 281.
Defines WDC (Ward Development Committee) - "the grass root link between
the communities and the Local Authorities", formed per ward via an
electoral process in which residents select representatives.
